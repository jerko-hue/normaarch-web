Studio

Norma Studio is an architecture and interior design office in Mostar, led by Jerko Petrović, MSc Civil Engineering (Architecture).

We design family houses, residential buildings, sports and tourism facilities, apartments and shops. We work across Herzegovina, and for clients abroad we also work remotely: meetings, drawings and visualisations online.

Every client sees the project before it is built: in images, film and drawings they understand. Decisions are made on time, and the site gets drawings it can build from.
