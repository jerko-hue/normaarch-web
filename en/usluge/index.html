Services

Everything from one office: architecture, permit documents and the interior down to the last socket.

Concept design

  • Site analysis and your brief
  • Plans, sections and elevations
  • Two rounds of changes included
  • Visualisations of the building
  • Documents for planning consent and location permit

Permit design

  • Documents for the building permit
  • Coordination of all disciplines: structure, electrical, mechanical, plumbing and drainage
  • Technical description and compliance with regulations
  • Drawings for contractors and bill of quantities

Interior design

  • Concept, materials and colours
  • Furniture layout and visualisations
  • Drawings of every wall, lighting and socket positions
  • Bespoke furniture for the joiner
  • Equipment list and cost estimate

Visualisation and film

  • Photorealistic exterior and interior images
  • Film for sales and presentation
  • Presentations for investors and buyers

Design supervision

  • Site visits
  • Coordination with contractors
  • Solutions for whatever comes up during construction

How we work

  1. QuestionnaireFill in the questionnaire or get in touch. We need the location, what you are building and roughly how big.
  2. QuoteWe send a quote with price and timeline. Each phase is paid half at the start, half on completion.
  3. ConceptPlans, elevations and visualisations. Two rounds of changes are included.
  4. Permit design or interior detailingDocuments for the permit, or drawings for contractors and joiners.
  5. SupervisionIf agreed, we follow construction until you move in.
